List for free. Pay only when it sells.

A CheckMyPlot risk check is required before anything can list, so every plot on BuyMyPlot has already been screened. Once your plot or estate passes, listing itself costs nothing.

How listing works

Three steps, whether it is one plot or an entire estate.

STEP 1

Pass CheckMyPlot first

Every plot must clear CheckMyPlot's Assurance check before anything can be submitted for listing. This is paid upfront by the seller, before a listing ever exists.

STEP 2

List for free

Once your plot or estate has passed, submit your details below. There is no listing fee, whether it is one plot or fifty.

STEP 3

Pay only when it sells

A 10% commission is due on any completed sale, 5% to MyPlot and 5% to Law Corridor for preparing the legal transfer documents.

What it costs

CheckMyPlot's fee is the only cost to list. Everything else is only owed if your plot actually sells.

BEFORE LISTING

CheckMyPlot Assurance

TO LIST

SellMyPlot listing fee

₦0. Free, for a single plot or a whole estate, once CheckMyPlot is passed.

ON SALE ONLY

Commission

10% of the sale price, split 5% to MyPlot and 5% to Law Corridor. Nothing is owed unless a sale actually completes.

NOTE

What counts as a plot

Each individually surveyed sub-parcel on the approved layout plan, the same definition CheckMyPlot uses.
