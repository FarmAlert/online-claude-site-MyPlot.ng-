Where your commission actually goes.

MyPlot's commission is not pure profit. It is due in full only once a sale completes, and it covers three things every completed sale genuinely requires, not just a platform fee for a listing.

What it covers

Three things, every one of them tied to actually getting a sale done safely.

ADMINISTRATION

Running the transaction

Our buyer relations team fields every inquiry, connects serious buyers to your listing, and coordinates the sale from first contact through to close, so you are not managing strangers directly.

LEGAL

Professional legal work

A qualified legal team reviews the transaction, advises on the paperwork, and stands behind the process, the same standard of legal diligence CheckMyPlot itself is built on.

DOCUMENTATION

Getting the paperwork right

Preparing, reviewing, and properly registering the documents a completed transfer requires, so the sale you close is one that actually holds up.

Why this matters

Most agents in Abuja charge a commission and disappear into the paperwork. We would rather you know what you are actually paying for.

01

Nothing upfront

Listing is free. The 10% is only ever due once a sale genuinely completes, never before.

02

Comparable to the market

In much of the Nigerian property market, legal fees, documentation, and administrative costs are billed separately from an agent's commission, often after the fact. MyPlot's 10% is designed to cover all three within a single, upfront number.

03

One number, no surprises

No separate legal invoice after the fact, no extra documentation fee that shows up at closing. What we quote is what is due.
