What Assurance checks

Every plot must clear these six sources before it can be listed. A mismatch between them is often the most valuable finding.

01 · DOCUMENTS

Document authentication

C of O, Deed of Assignment and Survey Plan checked for alteration or mismatched details.

02 · REGISTRY

AGIS & FCDA cross check

Ownership status, encumbrances and litigation flags checked against the official registry.

03 · SATELLITE

Baseline geospatial screening

Satellite imagery confirms the plot's boundary and location against its survey plan.

04 · ON THE GROUND

Community verification

Local checks on who is actually occupying or using the land.

05 · COURTS

Litigation records

Available court filings checked for disputes not yet reflected in the registry.

06 · REPORTS

Community fraud reports

Verified, anonymized reports from other buyers and agents on this plot or seller.

What Insight adds

A title can be genuine and the plot can still carry serious risk the registry will never show.

WATERWAYS

Drainage right of way

Checks whether the plot sits on or near a designated water channel, a common demolition trigger in the FCT.

FLOODING

Flood zone mapping

Historical and satellite based flood risk for the plot and its surrounding drainage capacity.

ZONING

Masterplan land use compliance

Confirms the plot is actually zoned for what it is being sold as.

CORRIDORS

High tension & pipeline check

Screens for proximity to power line easements or pipeline right of way.

SETBACKS

Road setback compliance

Checks whether the plot respects masterplan setback lines.

TERRAIN

Erosion & stability

Reviews slope and soil indicators relevant to construction risk.

What Foresight adds

For a significant financial decision, not just a safety decision.

VALUATION

Comparable market valuation

An estimate of fair value based on recent sale data for similar plots nearby.

FORECAST

Land value forecast

How the plot's value is likely to move, informed by nearby infrastructure and development plans.

POTENTIAL

Development potential

An assessment of rezoning or redevelopment upside for the plot.
