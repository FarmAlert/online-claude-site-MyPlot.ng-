Know what's really on that plot.

We triangulate documents, the AGIS and FCDA registries, satellite and land use history, on the ground checks, and community reports into one plain language risk report.

What we check

Every report covers six independent sources, so a mismatch between them is often the most valuable finding.

01 · DOCUMENTS

Document authentication

C of O, Deed of Assignment and Survey Plan checked for alteration or mismatched details.

02 · REGISTRY

AGIS & FCDA cross check

Ownership status, encumbrances and litigation flags checked against the official registry.

03 · SATELLITE

Land use history

Imagery over time surfaces flood zones, setbacks and contradictory land use.

04 · ON THE GROUND

Community verification

Local checks on who is actually occupying or using the land.

05 · COURTS

Litigation records

Available court filings checked for disputes not yet reflected in the registry.

06 · REPORTS

Community fraud reports

Verified, anonymized reports from other buyers and agents on this plot or seller.
